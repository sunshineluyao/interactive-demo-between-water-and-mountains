## Fonts

Bodoni Moda (500) and Source Sans 3 (400 and 600) are self-hosted in ../fonts. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included there. Font data was retrieved from Google Fonts; no runtime font request is made to a third party.

## Interaction tutorial excerpts

The files in `tutorial/` are reduced-resolution teaching excerpts. They keep the original authors’ copyright and are not relicensed under the application’s MIT license. Complete APA-style citations are visible in the website’s **References & credits** section.

- `munzner-taxonomy.jpg`, `munzner-parameters.jpg`, `munzner-highlight.jpg`, `munzner-linked-views.jpg`, and `munzner-detail-on-demand.jpg`: slides 3, 8, 17, 34, and 38 from Tamara Munzner’s public *Visualization Analysis & Design: Interactive Views (Chapters 11–12)* lecture slides, https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~tmm/talks/vad/VAD-interact.pdf. The pages were rendered and cropped only for legibility.
- `vega-brush-link.gif`, `vega-overview-detail.gif`, and `vega-crossfilter.gif`, plus still frames: teaching captures of the official Vega-Lite interactive example family, https://vega.github.io/vega-lite/examples/#interactive. Cited to Arvind Satyanarayan, Dominik Moritz, Kanit Wongsuphasawat, and Jeffrey Heer (2017).
- `datatoon-interface.png`: interface excerpt from Nam Wook Kim, Nathalie Henry Riche, Benjamin Bach, Guanpeng Xu, Matthew Brehmer, Ken Hinckley, Michael Pahud, Haijun Xia, Michael J. McGuffin, and Hanspeter Pfister (2019), https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300335.
- `falx-interface.png`: interface excerpt from Chenglong Wang, Yu Feng, Rastislav Bodik, Isil Dillig, Alvin Cheung, and Amy J. Ko (2021), https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445249.
- `data-formulator.gif` and `data-formulator-still.png`: interface teaching capture cited to Chenglong Wang, John Thompson, and Bongshin Lee (2024), https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2023.3326585.
