# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

INFOSCI 301 students at Duke Kunshan University, exploring data visualization during class and preparing a group project. The instructor curates the demonstration.

## Product Purpose

Connect Kunshan to the world through community-based learning. Put mapped waterways and precipitation near Kunshan in conversation with archaeological pottery evidence from the Mandara region. Students inspect sources, compare representations, validate a claim and bring a question back to a local community.

## Operating Context

Existing React/TypeScript/Vite application. Must remain deployable to Vercel by importing a Git repository. The requested experience is artistic, animated and humane; the user rejected dense dashboard cards and a Markdown-book presentation. The current instruction “please apply” authorizes applying Frontend Design, UI/UX Pro Max, Taste Skill and Impeccable to this existing application. It follows the discussion of a cultural exhibition with real credited imagery, bilingual typography, purposeful motion and local reflection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve all six chapter anchors, source inspection, OSM channel selection, NASA monthly timeline, Mandara qualitative teaching matrix, local XLSX import, four validation levels and the Ed Discussion team-claim export. Keep supplied open data and its rights separate from the application license. Do not publish a site or fabricate a deployed URL. A preview must be the running application or a clearly identified artifact, never a manually drawn board described as a screenshot.

## Brand Commitments

Name: 水山之间 · Between Water & Mountains. A bilingual place-based voice, with the Analects passage as a question-making lens. Do not attribute a Chinese philosophical identity to Mandara communities.

## Evidence on Hand

Bundled OSM extract (420 ways), NASA POWER monthly point precipitation (300 months, 2001–2025), and a paper-derived Mandara teaching summary. The original tDAR row-level spreadsheet is not bundled; the local importer remains available. No community testimony or claimed local impact has been supplied. Wikimedia photographs provide place context only, with author and license attribution.

## Product Principles

- Meet the place and people’s questions before operating a tool.
- Keep source, observation, interpretation and missing voices distinguishable.
- Every activity contributes to the same final group project.
- Comparison opens inquiry without implying equivalent histories or shared causes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The implementation target is readable bilingual content, keyboard controls, responsive layouts, visible focus, reduced motion and useful non-animated states. Conformance is not claimed without a complete audit.
